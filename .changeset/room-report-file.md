Handing in a Berita Acara now means uploading the signed, scanned file (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB, checked by its first bytes): one file per room report, required to submit, replaceable by a coordinator while the report is submitted and downloadable by whoever may view the report. The Berita Acara list submits per room, per subject or per day with a file for every room, three uploads at a time, and retries only the rooms that failed.
